About Us

公司介绍

MUJIN GLOBAL HARDWARE & TOOLS CO. L.L.C. is a Dubai-based supplier of construction materials, hardware tools, fasteners, safety products and industrial supplies. We support contractors, construction companies and industrial clients with reliable sourcing, competitive pricing, responsive coordination and practical procurement solutions.

MUJIN GLOBAL HARDWARE & TOOLS CO. L.L.C. 是一家位于迪拜的建筑材料、五金工具、紧固件、安全防护用品及工业物资供应商。我们为承包商、建筑公司及工业客户提供可靠寻源、有竞争力的价格、及时协调与高效采购解决方案。
